How a sweep works

1. Find prospects. A search for roofing companies in the market, then a gate that rules out the ones not worth a call: commercial only, too few reviews, no real local address, or a storm chaser passing through.

2. Audit their sites. Each prospect that passes gets its site opened on a simulated phone, timed, and checked against about thirty things a homeowner would notice. We visit slowly and politely and never fill in or send anything.

3. Rank the call list. Not by score. The best call is a prospect customers already find but whose leads slip away, because the problem is real and the fix is quick.

4. Draft findings. For the prospects you choose, the model ranks the problems costing them the most work, in plain language. A person picks the three the owner reads before anything becomes a report.

Describe a job

Same work, described in a sentence instead of clicked through step by step. The coordinator finds the prospects, audits their sites, waits for that to finish, retries anything that got stuck, and hands back the call list.

Worth using when the job takes several steps and you would rather not sit and watch it, especially a large market that runs for an hour. Use the buttons on the left instead when you know the one thing you want.

It can only use the same steps you can. Every number it reports came from an actual check, it cannot make one up, and it cannot contact anybody.
